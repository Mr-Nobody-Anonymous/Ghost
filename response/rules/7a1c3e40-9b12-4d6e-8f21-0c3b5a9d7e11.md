# AMSI or ETW Function Patch (Process Access to ntdll/amsi)

- Rule: `7a1c3e40-9b12-4d6e-8f21-0c3b5a9d7e11`
- Severity: high
- ATT&CK: T1685
- Tactic: unmapped
- Status: **drafted from the rule, not worked against a live alert**

## What fired

Detects a process obtaining writable/read-write access to amsi.dll or ntdll.dll, consistent with in-memory patching of AmsiScanBuffer or EtwEventWrite. A second selection covers remote threads started in those modules.

Event IDs in scope: 8, 10.

## Triage

Check these in order. The first one that is empty means the rule matched on a field the collector did not populate.

- `EventID`
- `TargetImage`
- `GrantedAccess`
- `SourceImage`

Check each field for presence before checking its value. A missing field means the collector never populated it, and a rule matching on nulls is quiet rather than empty.

## Containment

`isolate-host` - Isolate the host pending a decision that needs no further telemetry.

Do not close out on the containment action alone. The entry being removed does not answer how it got there.

## What to preserve

- A memory image of the target process
- The loaded-module list at the time of the alert
- The EDR/AV product logs for the host, if present

## When this is a false positive

Anti-cheat and DRM components patch their own process memory. They do it from their own process, with a signed image, at startup - the same telemetry but a stable parent.
